Our Core Identity

What is FLAME?

FLAME is a pioneering educational initiative conceived and initiated by N. Samsudheen. It serves as a bridge connecting modern educational resources with the talented students of Mannarkkad constituency, Kerala.

By designing custom scholarship frameworks, digital classrooms, and hands-on skill-development programs, FLAME is active in nurturing and touching the lives of over 50,000+ individuals—including 48,000+ students and 2,500+ teachers across 140+ schools—ensuring that quality education is accessible to every single corner of Mannarkkad.

Active Projects

Nurturing Excellence through Key Initiatives

FLAME deploys targeted programs to upgrade infrastructure, award scholarships, and bring advanced training direct to schools.

📝

CM Kids LP

Dedicated constituency-level preparation programs and study-kit distribution for the LSS (Lower Secondary Scholarship) Exam, a state-level competitive scholarship examination conducted by the Government of Kerala for Class 4 students.

📚

CM Kids UP

Expert-led coaching and guidance for the CM Kid’s USS (Upper Secondary Scholarship) Exam, a prestigious, merit-based exam for Class 7 students in Kerala.

🎓

NMMS Scholarship Training

Structured training programs for the National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship (NMMS) exam for Class 8 students, helping selected scholars from economically weaker sections secure a central government scholarship of ₹48,000.

🏛️

Advanced Competitive Exam Preparation

Specialized entrance guidance, mock tests, and mentor coordination for CUET, NCET, IPMAT, KLEE, and CLAT — securing admissions to premier Central Universities, IIMs, and National Law Universities.

🧠

Aptitude & Career Counselling

Constituency-wide diagnostic evaluation tests and personalized guidance seminars to help students across the constituency discover their true strengths and career paths.

🏫

School Infrastructure Development

Enhancing classroom environments and upgrading educational facilities to bridge the digital divide in government schools across the constituency.
