Our Core Identity

What is FLAME?

FLAME is a pioneering educational initiative conceived and initiated by Advocate N Samsudheen MLA. It serves as a bridge connecting modern educational resources with the talented students of Mannarkkad constituency, Kerala.

By designing custom scholarship frameworks, digital classrooms, and hands-on skill-development programs, FLAME guarantees that high-quality education is accessible to all, irrespective of background.
